# Intent

Editable at any time. Changes come through drift checks and are logged in `drift-notes.md`.

## Goal

What the user wants to get done, and why, in their words.

## For whom

Who uses it, roughly how many, inside or outside the company.

## Success

How we will know it worked.

## Non-goals

What this is deliberately not.

## Assumptions

What Flight Control inferred and stated instead of asking.

## Risks

What could go wrong.

## Open questions

What is still unknown, and who probably knows.
